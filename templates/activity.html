READING THE DATA

What each status means

Active
A server interaction or online presence was observed within the past 60 days, after the new-member grace period. This does not confirm event attendance.
Needs review
The latest observation is older than 60 days, after the grace period. Review context before taking any action.
Grace
The member joined less than 90 days ago and is still in the new-member grace period.
Unknown
The join date is missing, or there are no observations after the grace period. Missing data is not proof of inactivity.
Exempt
A staff member excluded this person from activity classification. Remove the exemption to resume classification; their observations are kept.

Names reflect the latest values observed by the bot. Server names and usernames can change or be duplicated; Discord IDs remain the permanent identifier. Historical names and detailed activity history are not stored.
